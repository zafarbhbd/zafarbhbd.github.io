About Me

I am a political scientist with a deep interest in the intersection of climate change, public policy, and social vulnerability. My research examines how communities — particularly in South Asia — perceive, respond to, and adapt to environmental and political challenges. I also apply data science and machine learning methods to social science questions.

Beyond academia, I am committed to open knowledge and civic engagement, and I regularly explore how computational tools can strengthen policy research.

Future Ambitions

I aspire to contribute to evidence-based climate policy in the Global South, publish impactful interdisciplinary research, and develop open-source tools that bridge data science and political science scholarship.

Research Interests

My work spans political science, environmental studies, and computational social science.

🌊

Climate Change & Coastal Vulnerability

Examining how communities in Bangladesh's coastal zones perceive climate risks and develop adaptation strategies in response to environmental change.

⚖️

Public Policy & Governance

Investigating policy design, implementation gaps, and governance challenges in developing country contexts, with a focus on South Asia.

🏕️

Forced Migration & Security

Analyzing the political and security implications of the Rohingya refugee crisis for Bangladesh, including regional stability and humanitarian dimensions.

👩‍💼

Gender & Labour Migration

Understanding the factors that shape female migration into the informal sector in Bangladesh, including economic, social, and structural drivers.

🤖

Machine Learning in Social Science

Applying machine learning techniques — including PCA, SMOTE, and deep learning — to social science problems such as fraud detection and policy analysis.
